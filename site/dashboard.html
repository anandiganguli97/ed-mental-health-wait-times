Hospital characteristics

Expanded-model coefficients are shown with 95% intervals. The model includes log certified beds, a yes/no psychiatric-unit indicator, county HPSA, and state fixed effects. CAHs are limited to 25 inpatient/swing beds, so their coefficient overlaps structurally with bed count.

These are exploratory associations, not causal effects. Bed counts use log(1 + count); HPSA is county-level; a valid county FIPS with no designation record is coded No. The outcome is a difference between separately reported medians, and the model explains about 5.8% of gap variation.
